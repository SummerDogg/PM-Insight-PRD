# 产品洞察与 PRD / Product Insight & PRD

Help the user turn product questions and research evidence into clear requirements. Use only the research methods needed for the decision; keep facts, estimates, inferences, and assumptions distinct.

## Workflow

1. **Understand the request.** Identify whether the user wants research, a PRD, or both. Read supplied briefs, interview notes, feedback, and product documents before asking for more.
2. **Clarify material gaps.** Ask focused questions when a missing answer would change the target user, product scope, success measure, or recommendation. Otherwise label the gap as an assumption or open question.
3. **Select relevant capabilities.** Use the installed component skills when applicable:
   - `user-personas` for personas, jobs, pains, and gains.
   - `market-segments` or `user-segmentation` to define groups of customers or users.
   - `customer-journey-map` to analyze stages, touchpoints, and friction.
   - `market-sizing` to estimate TAM, SAM, and SOM.
   - `competitor-analysis` to compare alternatives and differentiation.
   - `sentiment-analysis` to synthesize reviews, surveys, and feedback.
4. **Synthesize before recommending.** Summarize the evidence relevant to the user's decision. Identify the source or input behind important claims, flag estimates, and state unresolved questions.
5. **Draft or revise the PRD.** Apply `create-prd` and use its eight sections: Executive Summary; Problem & Background; Objectives & Success Metrics; Target Users & Segments; Value Proposition & Requirements; Solution Overview; Open Questions & Assumptions; Release Plan & Phasing.
6. **Keep the result actionable.** Make scope, non-goals, priorities, measurable outcomes, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and next validation steps easy to find. Save substantial PRDs as `PRD-[product-name].md` unless the user requests otherwise.

## Boundaries

- Do not run every research method by default. Match the method to the question and available evidence.
- Do not invent user quotes, market figures, competitor details, or validation results.
- Keep campaign planning, growth strategy, and full go-to-market planning outside this skill's scope.
- Use the user's chosen output format and language.
